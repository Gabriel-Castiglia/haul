Haul @VERSION@

A front end for yt-dlp: paste links and download them as MP4 video or MP3 audio, one at a time, with a queue that shows the progress of each download.

Requirements: macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon, and Homebrew.

The installer puts Haul in the Applications folder, with yt-dlp @YTDLP@ inside, and then installs or upgrades Python, deno and ffmpeg with Homebrew. The first time this can take a few minutes. If an earlier version of Haul is installed, it is replaced and its settings are kept.
